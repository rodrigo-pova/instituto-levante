Beyond volleyball drills and nutrition on the sand, Instituto Levante is deeply committed to connecting our youth with the vibrant arts and cultural life of Rio de Janeiro.

Last Thursday, courtesy of a generous invitation from a partner theater ensemble, 45 students attended an acclaimed children's musical in downtown Rio. For nearly 80% of the kids, it was their very first time setting foot inside a live theater.

### Unlocking Curiosity & Imagination

The journey began on the chartered bus, filled with excited chatter as kids tried to guess what the stage set would look like. Stepping into the historic auditorium with soft lighting and red velvet seats, awe quietly filled the room.

After the performance, the cast came down to the orchestra to meet the children, answer questions about stagecraft and costumes, and encourage them to read the book that inspired the play — copies of which are now available for checkout in our beach library.

Sport, literacy, and culture move forward hand in hand at Levante!
