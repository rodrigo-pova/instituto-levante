Last Saturday, Ipanema Beach hosted one of the most meaningful milestones in our organization's journey: the **Instituto Levante 4-Year Anniversary Tournament**.

More than 100 children and teens gathered across co-ed age brackets, competing with tremendous athletic spirit, kindness, and teamwork. The championship recognized not just match points, but sportsmanship, fair play, and consistent engagement in our reading circles.

### Powered by Community Solidarity

This tournament was brought to life through a grassroots crowdfunding campaign that rallied neighbors, families, local businesses, and donors across Brazil. Thanks to this shared effort, every child received a commemorative medal, a special celebratory lunch with fresh fruits and smoothies, and official tournament jerseys.

> "Watching the beach packed with cheering families, students proudly holding medals, and reading books between scrimmages is living proof of what community sport can achieve." — _Mariana Souza, Co-founder_

Our deepest gratitude goes to every contributor who made this day unforgettable.
